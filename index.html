Privacy Policy for Astro Escape

Last updated: November 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Android game Astro Escape (package com.rmbgamingcompany.ImpostorRun, "the game", "we", "us") handles information. We do not require you to create an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address or phone number.

1. Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress locally on your device: coins, best score, unlocked skins and backgrounds, settings and whether you removed ads. This data stays on your device and is not sent to us. Uninstalling the game removes it.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads provided by Google AdMob, including interstitial and rewarded video ads. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information and ad interaction data to show and measure ads and prevent fraud. This data is handled by Google under Google's Privacy Policy. You can reset or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads). Players who purchase "Remove Ads" no longer see interstitial ads.

3. In-app purchases

Purchases (Remove Ads and coin packs) are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details. We only receive confirmation that a purchase was completed so the item can be delivered. The game uses Unity services to support these purchases.

4. Analytics and technical data

The game is made with the Unity engine. Unity and its services may process limited technical data, such as device type, operating system and app version, to make the game and purchases work. We do not use this to identify you personally.

5. Sharing

When you use the Share button, the game creates a picture of your score and opens the Android share sheet. The picture goes only to the app you choose.

6. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will help.

7. Data retention and deletion

We do not keep personal data on our own servers. Locally saved game data can be deleted by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the game. For data held by Google (ads and purchases), use your Google account controls.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The latest version will always be available at this page, with the date updated above.
